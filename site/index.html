Bosun In development

Assigns the work.
Then follows up.

Most project tools are a place to write work down. Bosun does the two things a board cannot: it turns a vague objective into specific assigned tasks, and then it follows up with the people who owe you something.

The question it answers

What did I delegate that is quietly not happening?

Not “what is everyone working on” — you can already see that. The expensive gap is the task you handed off three weeks ago that nobody has touched and nobody has mentioned.

How it works

Four steps. Only the first one needs you.

  1. State the goal

    A rough objective is enough. “Ship the Q4 pricing page.” No structure required.

  2. Answer the questions

    Bosun asks what a good chief of staff would ask before planning — scope, constraints, who signs off. Your answers become the context it plans against.

  3. Tasks land with names on them

    Not a backlog. Each task carries one assignee, a deadline, an estimate, and a status you can act on.

  4. It follows up without you

    On each person's cadence, in the tone you set. Replies come back as status updates attached to the task, not buried in a thread.

Everything it does, on the record

Bosun acts on your behalf, so every action it takes is written down where you can see it. This is what a week looks like.

  • goal analyzed
    Read “Ship the Q4 pricing page”

    Found three unstated assumptions. Asked about them.

  • clarification asked
    Asked who signs off on the copy

    Held the plan back until it had an answer.

  • tasks generated
    Split the goal into 7 tasks

    Each one small enough to finish in a day.

What it isn't

Being clear about this is most of the product.

Not a board

There are columns, but they are the least interesting thing here. A board nobody updates is the problem, not the fix.

Not a chatbot

You do not prompt it. It works from a goal and a schedule, and it starts the conversations.

Not a surveillance tool

It tracks commitments, not people. Nothing here measures keystrokes, hours, or activity.

Not autonomous

It drafts, assigns, and asks. Every action is logged, and every one of them is yours to reverse.
